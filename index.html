Monthly sponsorship – Port-au-Prince, Haiti

Adopt a school in Haiti

$25 a month keeps teachers paid and children fed.

Since the collapse of their building in the 2010 earthquake, the United Evangelical School in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, has operated in temporary facilities, in harsh conditions.

PEER Servants Canada runs a sponsorship program to help the school continue its basic operations until the new building is ready.

For a minimum of $25 per month, sponsors help keep the school open and running, paying teachers and providing food for the children.

Pregnancy and childbirth centre – Cotonou, Benin

Mother-Child Medical Clinic in Cotonou

Quality, affordable care for mothers and babies in an underserved area.

With the aim of helping the population of a disadvantaged area of Cotonou, Peer Servants Canada is pursuing a project to establish a Mother-Child Medical Clinic in partnership with the Beninese association founded by Maman Tamou.

The clinic will offer the local population quality and accessible healthcare services. The maternity ward will offer prenatal consultations for pregnant women, pregnancy-related healthcare, pregnancy monitoring, and more. Our continuous, equitable, and high-quality services will be fast, efficient, less invasive, and affordable for the general population.

The clinic will include

  • Maternity ward
  • Pharmacy
  • Laboratory
  • Medical imaging unit

About us

Helping people experience economic, social, and spiritual transformation.

Vision

To help people experience economic, social, and spiritual transformation.

Mission

To transform lives by partnering for economic empowerment and renewal through education and leadership development.

Our objectives

Education

To advance education by establishing and operating schools for underprivileged children in Haiti and in developing countries in Africa.

Public health

To develop and promote public health in Haiti and developing countries in Africa by educating the public on disease prevention and curative measures, and by organizing medical clinics for the underprivileged.

Leadership

To develop community leadership by providing workshops and training seminars to local church and community leaders in Haiti and developing countries in Africa.
